# HealthTech Payer Services — Medical Policy

**Document ID:** HT-POL-001 | **Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 | **Version:** 2.0 (CURRENT)

**STATUS: CURRENT / APPROVED** | **CONFIDENTIALITY: INTERNAL**

## 1. Comprehensive Inpatient & Outpatient Coverage Policy

This policy defines the coverage criteria, pre-authorization guidelines, and medical necessity thresholds for inpatient and outpatient clinical procedures under HealthTech Benefit Plans.

### 1.1 Clinical Necessity Principles

Services are considered medically necessary only if provided in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice and supported by objective clinical evidence.

#### 1.1.A Inpatient Level of Care Criteria

- **Acute Surgical Intervention:** Requires 24-hour continuous skilled nursing care post-procedure.
- **Severe Comorbidity Management:** Active management of organ system failure or high-risk systemic instability.
- **Observation Standard:** Outpatient observation status must be utilized for stays expected to be under 24 hours.

#### 1.1.B Outpatient Surgical & Diagnostic Services

1. **Prior Authorization Requirements:** High-tech diagnostic imaging (CPT 70000–79999) requires pre-certification within 72 hours of scheduled service.
2. **Facility Setting Tiering:** Elective outpatient procedures performed in an Ambulatory Surgical Center (ASC) receive standard 100% allowed benefit; hospital-based outpatient departments require secondary medical director approval.

## 2. Exclusions & Limitations

Services determined to be experimental, investigational, or cosmetic are strictly non-covered unless specifically mandated by regional statutory provisions or approved via the Clinical Exception Committee (HT-PROC-001).
