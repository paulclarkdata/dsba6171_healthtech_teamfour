DOCUMENT ID: HT-REG-001
TITLE: Federal Payer Compliance & Prompt Pay Standards
AUTHORITY SOURCE: Public / CMS Guidance Guidelines
EFFECTIVE DATE: May 15, 2023
JURISDICTION: US Federal
CONFIDENTIALITY: Public

1. PROMPT PAYMENT MANDATE
Under federal compliance standards, health plans and payer organizations must process and adjudicate clean claims within 30 calendar days of electronic receipt. Clean claims are defined as submissions possessing all required data elements specified in HIPAA X12 837 transaction standards.

2. ADVERSE ACTION & DENIAL NOTIFICATIONS
Every claim denial, partial allowance, or adverse benefit determination requires a written Explanation of Benefits (EOB) provided to the member and provider within 15 business days of adjudication.
The notification must explicitly state:
  a. Specific plan provision, policy code, or medical guideline relied upon (e.g., HT-POL-001).
  b. Right to internal appeal within 180 days.
  c. External review availability for clinical necessity denials.
