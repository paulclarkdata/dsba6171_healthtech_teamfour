Q1: How should an auditor handle a claim where billed amount exceeds allowed fee in HT-GUIDE-002?
A1: The system should automatically adjust the charge to the contracted allowed amount. The provider balance is written off under network contractual agreements unless out-of-network rules apply.

Q2: What is the correct protocol when a member's eligibility status is 'INACTIVE' on the service date?
A2: Deny claim immediately under Code ERR_ELIG_01. Link claim event to patient_accounts.csv eligibility status record.

Q3: How do we audit unbundled procedure codes?
A3: Refer to National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edit tables embedded in procedure_catalog.csv. If modifier -59 is absent, flag for unbundling review under HT-PROC-001.
